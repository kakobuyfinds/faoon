How to use a KakoBuy spreadsheet

A spreadsheet brings product links, variants and research notes into one place. This page presents a small CAFANS selection in a browsable format; it does not place orders or confirm that every item is supported by a particular agent.

01 / DISCOVER

Find the exact listing

Use the category filters to narrow the selection. Open the matching CAFANS page and record the product link, selected color, size and quantity. Similar photographs can refer to different options.

02 / COMPARE

Resolve the missing details

Compare measurements with an item you already own. Confirm the seller’s stated materials, current price and return conditions. Keep unconfirmed details visible in your notes.

03 / REVIEW

Check the ordering route

On the source page, check which agent options are available. If choosing KakoBuy, confirm that the original product link and variant are accepted before paying. Review the final charges and applicable terms.

Four checks before you commit

Fit & measurements

For hoodies and sweatshirts, compare chest width, shoulder width, sleeve length and body length using the same measurement method. For footwear, confirm the sizing system and the seller’s measurement guidance.

Listing photos & QC

Seller photographs show the advertised item. If warehouse QC images become available, check the received size label, color, quantity and visible condition. Photos alone do not establish authenticity or material composition.

Shipping & parcel size

Ask for a destination-specific quote with the packed dimensions and weight. Check whether the selected service uses actual or volumetric weight and what the quoted amount includes.

Returns & timing

Confirm the applicable return deadline, any return cost and the agent’s current storage conditions. Save the terms with your order notes and review issues while the relevant options remain available.

KakoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a KakoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product-link list used to research items for possible ordering through KakoBuy. Useful rows include the source link, variant, quoted price, measurement information and notes. This page is an independent guide with a CAFANS catalog selection.

Do the main buttons open KakoBuy?

No. “KakoBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” both open CAFANS. Individual product cards open their matching CAFANS detail pages. Confirm available agent options on the source listing.

Are the displayed products real CAFANS listings?

Yes. The six product names, listing images, reference prices and matching source links were imported from the CAFANS homepage. This selection is static; the source determines current availability and pricing.

Does the reference price include international shipping?

Do not assume it does. The imported amounts are estimated USD product prices from CAFANS. Confirm shipping, service fees, payment charges and any applicable taxes separately before relying on a total.

Are these products QC verified?

This page has not independently inspected the products. The displayed images are source listing images, and no QC rating or authenticity guarantee is assigned here.

Can I browse without creating an account here?

Yes. This page requires no account to view the selection, search products, filter categories or use the calculator. External services may have their own account requirements.

What should I save in my own spreadsheet?

Save the product name, source URL, seller if known, variant, quantity, price and currency, checked date, measurements, QC notes and separately quoted charges. Mark missing information as unknown so it remains easy to follow up.
